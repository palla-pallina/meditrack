# WORKFLOW
```
Doctor prescribes to patient off-platform
    │
    ▼
Head Nurse records Digital Prescription
    │
    ▼
Pharmacy validates medication
    │
    ▼
Head Nurse assigns medication schedule
    │
    ▼
Nurse sees assigned schedules
    │
    ▼
Nurse administers medication
    │
    ▼
Administration record saved
    │
    ▼
Medication Event (optional)
        ├── Missed Dose
        ├── Allergy
        ├── Overdose
        └── Interaction
```
