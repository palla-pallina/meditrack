#### Record Patient Admission Form
1. Clerk logs into their home dashboard and clicks ‘Record Patient Admission Form’
2. Clerk enters the required data
3. Clerk checks the entered details before saving the admission form

#### Create Prescription (Doctor)
1. Doctor logs into their home dashboard and clicks ‘Add Digital Prescription’
2. Doctor enters the required info.
3. Doctor checks the entered digital prescription details before saving

#### Assign Dispenser Compartment (Admin)
1. Admin logs in and accesses the MediTrack dispenser module then clicks ‘Create Compartment’
2. Admin selects the dispenser compartment that will be assigned to a patient.
3. Admin selects the patient the compartment will be assigned to (only patients with prescriptions).
4. The system will automatically allocate medicines to compartment slots based on schedule and availability.
5. Upon saving, the carousel’s status will be set as “Low”.

Note: The carousel contains 21 physical slots. Each slot can contain 1–4 medicines scheduled for the same administration time. 

#### Refill Dispenser (Pharmacist)
1. Pharmacist accesses the MediTrack dispenser module
2. The pharmacist will select which carousel (compartment) will be refilled
3. The pharmacist will set the amount in which the carousel will be filled
4. Upon saving, the carousel’s status will be set as “Full”

#### Assign Medication Administration (Head Nurse)
1. Head nurse logs in and enters their home dashboard, then clicks ‘Assign Medication Administration to Nurse’
2. Head nurse selects the prescription
3. Head nurse selects the assigned nurse
4. Head nurse enters the required info
5. Head Nurse checks the entered administration assignment details before saving

#### Dispense Medicine (Nurse) - MOBILE
1. Nurse logs into the mobile app
2. 15 minutes before the scheduled administration, the nurse will receive a notification
3. In the mobile app, nurse navigates to the scheduled medication administration and clicks ‘Begin Administering’
4. The system will ask the nurse if there is a receiving tray in the MediTrack dispenser unit (ex. ‘Place the receiving tray in the dispenser opening’ -> to which the nurse will click either ‘Receiving tray is placed’ or ‘Cancel’)
5. The system will display the scheduled administration’s info (ex. Patient, Room, Medicines to be dispensed, etc.). The nurse will confirm if the info is correct.
6. The MediTrack unit will begin to dispense the prescribed medication as required. Once successful, the system will confirm to the nurse that the medicine was successfully dispensed.
7. The system will idle and wait for an update on the medication administration from the nurse.
8. The nurse will proceed to the patient to administer the medicine.

#### View Activity Logs (Admin)
1. Admin logs in and clicks the Activity Logs module in their dashboard.
2. Admin clicks on a log to display more information.
